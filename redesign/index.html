Four redesign directions

Proposals, not finals. Each is a clickable prototype — home, films, and the Kherson 2023 reportage — built from the real content and the existing palette. The variants differ in the idea, not the layout: what is the one thing a visitor carries out the door?

A · time & sequence

The Cut

The site behaves like an edit: full-bleed frames, hard scroll-snap cuts, a frame counter in the corner. You move through the work in the order she cut it.

Remembered: "her site doesn't scroll — it cuts."

B · the image as object

One Wall

A dark viewing room: one work hangs at a time, matted in a hairline frame with a proper wall label, and you walk past the works. The grid is banned everywhere.

Remembered: standing in front of one photograph, properly, in the dark.

C · radically typographic

Sentence First

Her rule — "if the idea does not work as a sentence, no amount of rendering will save it" — becomes the interface. Works are loglines set huge; the image rises behind the words only as proof.

Remembered: a sentence, and the filmmaker who writes first.

D · identity as architecture

Two Ways

"I make images two ways — with a camera, and with a machine that dreams." The home is one diptych with a draggable seam between a photograph and an AI frame; the seam runs through every page.

Remembered: dragging the line and watching the photograph become the dream.
