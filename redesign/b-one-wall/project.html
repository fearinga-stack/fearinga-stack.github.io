Kherson, 2023

Documentary photography · eleven prints

The breach of Kakhovka dam resulted in flooding cities and villages on both banks of Dnipro and caused humanitarian catastrophe. Some villages were left without water: access to some of the cities and villages is still blocked because of the floods. Inhabitants are left without food, pot water and even technical water supply is limited.

I — Kherson, June 2023
