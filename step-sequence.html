Step Sequence

2026 · SHORT· DRAMA · 5.5MIN

Official Nominee — OMNI Ningbo · Official Selection — NOMOREFROG AI Film Awards 2026

Years after putting her own dream away, a woman returns to the rink where it started — and has to decide whether joy is still worth the risk.

watch

There are dreams we lose. And there are dreams we put away ourselves.

After years of living the life she was supposed to want, Rita returns to the place where everything began. Among old memories, empty ice rinks and unfinished sketches, she faces the question she has spent years avoiding: what if joy is worth the risk?

A short film about memory, identity, and finding your way back to yourself.

In figure skating, a step sequence is a required element: a run of turns and edges performed across the full length of the ice. Every step is counted. Rita's life has a step sequence too — and she performed it exactly as required.

PROCESS

The film is named after a figure skating element, so the skating had to be right. A step sequence has a defined structure — a run of turns and edges performed across the ice — and anyone who knows the sport reads a wrong one instantly. So the movement wasn't left to chance: I used Kling's motion control to direct each skating shot, specifying the path, the direction changes, and the way the body carries through them.

Video was generated in Kling and Seedance. Stills and character references were built in Nano Banana Pro. The film was assembled, graded and finished in DaVinci Resolve.
